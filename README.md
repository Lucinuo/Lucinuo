# Lucinuo

Lucille Huang’s public digital studio for biological research, formally published work, selected projects, and open implementation.

[Website](https://lucinuo.github.io/) · [Research](https://lucinuo.github.io/research/) · [Publications](https://lucinuo.github.io/publications/) · [Projects](https://lucinuo.github.io/projects/)

## Research

My current work connects experimental biology, platelet signaling, bioactive compounds, evidence synthesis, research visualization, and scientific communication. I aim to keep each scientific claim traceable to what the underlying evidence can actually support.

## Selected public work

- **[Lighthouse](https://lucinuo.github.io/projects/lighthouse/)** — a public concept prototype for a quiet companionship application, exploring how two people can signal presence without creating another notification stream. The interactive concept is available to view.
- **[Phase Shift](https://lucinuo.github.io/projects/phase-shift/)** — an original browser game: a competitive falling-block puzzle against a computer opponent, where clearing rows charges a phase meter that can become either an attack or a guard. [Playable in the browser](https://lucinuo.github.io/phase-shift/).
- **[Bearing](https://lucinuo.github.io/projects/bearing/)** — a private application for personal reflection and direction. Its design concept and decision principles are public; private login, the application, and its data are not open for public use.
- **[Lucinuo Website System](https://github.com/Lucinuo/lucinuo.github.io)** — the public website, shared design system, and deployment source.
