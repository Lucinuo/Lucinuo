Hey there 👋🏻

I'm a PhD candidate at **National Defense Medical University, Taiwan**, investigating how **fucoidan** — a sulfated polysaccharide from *Saccharina japonica* — rewires early platelet signaling to accelerate burn wound healing. My work combines wet-lab biochemistry with phosphoproteomic profiling to map the receptor-level mechanisms that actually matter.

---

### 🔬 What I'm Working On

- Mapping the **NRP-1 → FAK → Akt** axis in thermally injured vascular tissue
- Running phosphoproteomic screens to uncover novel signaling nodes downstream of **GPVI**
- Building Python & R pipelines for pathway analysis (IPA + custom scripts)
- PhD Year 3, QE ✓, and still going ☕

---

### 🧬 Research

<p>
  <a href="https://lucinuo.github.io"><img width="220" align="right" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/→_Read_more-lucinuo.github.io-blueviolet?style=for-the-badge"></a>
</p>

My research sits at the intersection of **vascular biology** and **translational medicine**.

Fucoidan has known anti-inflammatory and pro-hemostatic properties — but the receptor-level mechanisms in the context of burn injury are largely unmapped. I'm building that map.

Key questions:
- How does SJF interact with **GPVI** and **NRP-1** on platelet surfaces?
- What phosphorylation cascades does it trigger in thermally damaged tissue?
- Can we design a fucoidan-based intervention that measurably improves hemostasis in burn wounds?
