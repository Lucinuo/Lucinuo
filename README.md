---

I'm a PhD candidate at **National Defense Medical University**, Taiwan, studying the molecular mechanisms by which fucoidan — a sulfated polysaccharide from *Saccharina japonica* — modulates early platelet activation in burn wound healing. My work combines receptor-level biochemistry with phosphoproteomic profiling to map the signaling architecture of thermally injured vasculature.

Current focus: the **NRP-1 → FAK → Akt** axis as a candidate mediator of fucoidan's hemostatic effect, alongside GPVI signaling and P-selectin dynamics in platelet early activation.

---

**Methods**

Platelet isolation · Light transmission aggregometry · Flow cytometry (CD62P, PAC-1) · Western blot · Phosphoproteomics · Murine burn models · Pathway analysis (IPA)

---

**Computational & knowledge infrastructure**

I work at the boundary of wet lab and data analysis. Python and R for phosphoproteomic pipelines; Obsidian for interconnected literature and hypothesis networks; Zotero for reference management. I think of these as a system, not a stack.

---

**Current directions**

- Phosphoproteomic mapping of SJF-stimulated platelet signaling
- NRP-1 as a candidate fucoidan receptor in thermally injured tissue
- Translational potential of marine-derived polysaccharides in wound care
